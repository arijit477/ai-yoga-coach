## 1. Pose Pipeline

The computer vision to speech pipeline maintains a strict responsibility boundary between local computer vision evaluation and OpenAI speech synthesis:

```
[Camera Stream (30–60 FPS)]
       │
       ▼
[MediaPipe Tasks Vision] (33 3D Landmarks extracted locally on GPU)
       │
       ▼
[PoseEvaluator & RuleEngine] (Deterministic angle calculations & bound checks)
       │
       ▼ (PoseEvaluationResult)
[CoachingEventEngine] (Camera guard, delta tracking, milestone detection)
       │
       ▼ (CoachingEvent)
[CoachDecisionEngine] (Priority arbitration, 4s baseline & 10s repeat cooldowns)
       │
       ▼ (Approved CoachingEvent)
[RealtimeVoiceAgent] (DataChannel: response.create)
       │
       ▼ (WebRTC Audio Stream - 24kHz)
[OpenAI Realtime API] (gpt-realtime-2.1-mini)
       │
       ▼
[Web Audio API (Gain 2.5x + AnalyserNode)] ──► [Speakers & Avatar Lip-Sync]
```

---

## 2. Rule Evaluation

- **Deterministic Evaluation:** Pose alignment is evaluated against explicit geometric rules (joint angle ranges, limb extensions, verticality, horizontality) declared in the Asana Rule Registry.
- **Single Source of Truth:** Computer vision and mathematical rule evaluation dictate posture accuracy. OpenAI **never** evaluates joint angles or decides whether an asana is correct.

---

## 3. Confidence Handling & Occlusion Filtering

- **Camera Guard:** In [`CoachingEventEngine.ts`](file:///c:/Users/prabi/OneDrive/Desktop/arijit/ai-yoga-coach/frontend/src/features/ai-coach/voice/CoachingEventEngine.ts), posture corrections are strictly disabled unless `cameraState === "CAMERA_READY"`.
- **Occlusion vs Posture Mistake:** If a limb or body part leaves the frame (`PARTIAL_BODY` or `NO_PERSON`), the engine emits camera visibility prompts (e.g. *"Take a step back so I can see your full posture"*) rather than generating false joint-angle corrections.

---

## 4. Temporal Stability

- **Landmark & Score Smoothing:** Raw MediaPipe landmarks pass through temporal moving averages to reject single-frame tracking noise and jitter.
- **Debounced Triggers:** Brief tracking anomalies (1–2 frames) are absorbed by the temporal evaluator and never reach the voice layer. Only persistent alignment issues trigger coaching events.

---

## 5. Coaching Events

Structured, lightweight objects generated by `CoachingEventEngine`:

```typescript
interface CoachingEvent {
  id: string;
  type: CoachingEventType;
  asanaId: string;
  asanaName: string;
  score?: number;
  primaryIssue?: {
    ruleId: string;
    joint?: string;
    severity: "low" | "medium" | "high";
    feedback: string;
  };
  feedback?: string;
  timestamp: number;
}
```

## 6. Decision Engine & Single-Correction Prioritization

[`CoachDecisionEngine.ts`](file:///c:/Users/prabi/OneDrive/Desktop/arijit/ai-yoga-coach/frontend/src/features/ai-coach/voice/CoachDecisionEngine.ts) acts as the central gatekeeper:
- If multiple posture errors occur simultaneously (e.g. knee, shoulder, and hips), the engine selects the **single highest-priority issue**.
- The practitioner receives **one clear cue at a time** (e.g. *"Sink your front knee deeper"*), preventing mental and sensory overload.

---

## 7. Priority Hierarchy

| Priority | Event Type | Description |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | `safety_warning` | Immediate preemption of any ongoing speech. |
| **2–4** | `camera_unavailable`, `user_out_of_frame`, `partial_body` | Prompts user to adjust camera position. |
| **6** | `pose_correction` | Actionable physical alignment corrections. |
| **7** | `issue_improving`, `issue_resolved` | Immediate micro-validation on posture adjustment. |
| **8** | `pose_completed` | Single celebratory completion announcement per pose. |
| **9** | `good_form` | Alignment affirmation (governed by 6s cooldown). |
| **10** | `pose_started`, `calibration_prompt`, `pose_held` | Routine lifecycle cues. |

---

## 8. Cooldown & Anti-Spam

- **Global Baseline Cooldown (`4,000ms`):** Minimum interval between any two consecutive voice events.
- **Identical Rule Suppression (`10,000ms`):** Prevents repeating the exact same correction if the user has not yet adjusted.
- **Good-Form Cooldown (`6,000ms`):** Prevents repetitive praise spam.
- **Good-Form Silence:** When alignment is correct, the coach remains quiet, allowing peaceful breathing.

---

## 9. Recovery Detection

- When the practitioner adjusts their form into alignment, `CoachingEventEngine` detects `!primary && this.activeIssue` and emits `issue_resolved`.
- `CoachDecisionEngine` immediately clears `ruleTimestamps` for that rule, rewarding the user with positive reinforcement.

---

## 10. Regression Detection

- Because `issue_resolved` clears the rule timestamp, if the practitioner later loses alignment and regresses into the same mistake, the system treats it as a new event and can provide the correction again after the standard cooldown.

---

## 11. Asana Context & Seamless Transitions

- **Per-Asana Scoping:** All rule trackers, milestone sets, and completion flags are strictly scoped to the active asana.
- **Transition Cleanup:** When switching from Warrior II to Tree Pose (or resetting the session), `coachingEngineRef.current.reset()` and `voiceResetEngine()` are invoked immediately, guaranteeing that stale corrections from the previous pose are never spoken during the new pose.

---

## 12. Left / Right & Mirroring Consistency

- MediaPipe landmark coordinates are normalized anatomical representations.
- Cue strings explicitly refer to the anatomical body part (e.g. *"Sink your front knee deeper"*, *"Lift your chest"*), maintaining complete consistency across mirrored camera views.

---

## 13. Voice Payload

- Only concise text instructions with prosody tags are transmitted over the WebRTC DataChannel (e.g. `instructions: "Speak this concise yoga coaching cue: 'Sink your front knee deeper.'"`).
- Zero landmark coordinates, joint angles, or frame buffers are transmitted to OpenAI.

---

## 14. OpenAI Responsibility

- **Role:** Natural prosody, humanistic British vocal delivery, and lifelike cadence.
- **Constraints:** OpenAI does not evaluate posture, does not ask questions, does not invite verbal dialogue, and does not invent unapproved corrections.
